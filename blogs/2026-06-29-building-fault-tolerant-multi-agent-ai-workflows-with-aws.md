This article explains how Lambda durable functions enable orchestration of complex AI agent workflows by providing automatic checkpointing and replay mechanisms. The post demonstrates a healthcare prior authorization pipeline coordinating multiple agents, human reviews, and payer submissions while handling failures gracefully without re-running completed steps.
